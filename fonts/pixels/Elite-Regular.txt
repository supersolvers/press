Draft hand-tuned pixels for Elite Regular's Greek and Cyrillic, to be reviewed and merged into
fonts-of-old/pixels/Elite-Regular.txt. Same format as that file: the cell is 8 px wide and 21 rows,
the baseline sits below row 16, capitals start at row 7, small letters at row 10.

How they are drawn: stems are two pixels, as in the rest of Elite. A letter with three stems, or with a
stem inside a bowl, has no room for that in seven columns, so its middle stem is one pixel (as the
hand-tuned M and W already do) and it uses all seven columns. Letters that are the same shape as a
hand-tuned Latin one copy it.

merged into fonts-of-old/pixels/Elite-Regular.txt. Same format: the cell is 8 px wide and 21 rows,

solid block in an 8 px cell. Where a stroke has no room to be two pixels it is one. k is an ASCII
letter that was not hand-tuned yet and comes out heavy; it is here too, so that ķ and ǩ follow it.
